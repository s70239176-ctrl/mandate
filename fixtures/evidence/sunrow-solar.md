# Sunrow Energy: project update

Sunrow Energy builds and operates utility-scale solar farms and grid batteries.

Its Meridian plant, a 300 MW photovoltaic farm with 100 MWh of battery storage, began commercial operation this year and sells electricity under a 15-year power purchase agreement.

Independent audits of every Sunrow site report no labor-rights violations and no safety incidents. Revenue grew 18 percent year on year, and the company has no outstanding regulatory actions.
